# Changelog

All notable changes to Linkerter are documented here. Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [2.0.0] - 2026-10-22

### Added

- Shared canonical post identity, safe DOM/message/storage primitives, and a real Node/jsdom test harness.
- Multilingual English, French, and Arabic classifier corpus evaluation with polarity/negation handling and explainable evidence.
- Single-writer `off`, `session`, and `persistent` history modes, coordinated writes, retention, provenance, and safe record merging.
- Incremental feed monitoring, bounded extraction/expansion, keyed paginated results, search/sort/details, and local review labels.
- Schema-v2 JSON/CSV exports, field selection, sanitized diagnostic downloads, and distinct history/all-data deletion controls.
- Local EN/FR/AR popup dictionaries, Arabic RTL, responsible-use onboarding, keyboard tabs, reduced motion, and accessible reflow/focus styling.
- Real-Chrome MV3 integration harness with sanitized intercepted HTTPS fixtures, concurrent-tab/storage/reload/download/keyboard scenarios, and an explicit unavailable-browser gate.
- 65-case adversarial EN/FR/AR corpus (`tests/fixtures/classifier/adversarial.json`) covering real vacancy formats, opening-shaped lookalikes, negation, mixed clauses, recruiter searches, job seekers, advice, and closed posts.
- `npm run probe:classifier` reports per-category precision/recall and `open_role` false positives; `npm run probe:feed` proves one realistic card yields one post root, one archive record, and chrome-free text. Both gate with `--strict` and run strict in CI.
- Post age and an explicit “older than 60 days, may no longer be active” marker reach the result list, so a months-old reshared opening is visibly separated from a current one.
- Branded 16/48/128 PNG icons, privacy/support/security/contribution documentation, store drafts, deterministic unpacked/store packagers, and CI.
- `npm run preview:popup` serves the real popup inside an 800×600 frame — Chrome's hard popup ceiling — with a synthetic `chrome.*` stub and in-memory records, plus a **Before this change** link that renders the previous stylesheet for A/B comparison (`scripts/preview-popup.cjs`, `scripts/preview-stub.js`). Development-only: it is never packaged and never contacts LinkedIn.
- `npm run dev:auto-update` (`scripts/dev-auto-update.mjs`) plus a development-only `background.js` hook: the watcher polls the GitHub branch, fast-forwards only a clean checkout, stamps the commit in a gitignored `dev-autoreload.json`, and the unpacked extension reloads itself and its LinkedIn tabs. No new permission, no packaged code path, and uncommitted work is never overwritten.

- A 26-case real-world classifier corpus (`tests/fixtures/classifier/real-world.json`) taken from the first authenticated export, sanitized and independently labeled. It joined the pinned and adversarial corpora in one gate: `npm run probe:classifier -- --strict` now reports accuracy **1.000** (113/113), **0** `open_role` false positives and **0** false negatives, precision/recall 1.0 for every category, and per-language en 46/46, fr 32/32, ar 13/13.
- `tests/fixtures/dom/feed-2026.html` reproduces the card shape the live feed actually served rather than the shape that had been assumed: no `data-urn` anywhere, the post URN only in the permalink anchor, `update-components-*` body and actor markup, absolute French timestamps, an actor headline, reaction counts, a comment thread, a reactor wrapper around a shared post, and a sponsored placement. `tests/dom/monitor-feed-2026.test.js` (8 tests) pins identity, authorship, chrome-free text, completeness, publish date, reshare deduplication, engagement scoping and sponsored exclusion against it.

- An executable fixture-sanitization policy (`tests/unit/fixture-sanitization.test.js`, 4 tests): fixtures derived from a real export must use placeholder profile and company slugs, reserved `.example` mailboxes and hosts, synthetic job ids, and must carry no member tracking parameter, LinkedIn safety redirect or phone number — and no fixture at all may carry a profile URL ending in a real numeric or hex member id. `tests/fixtures/README.md` documents the policy and lists the real-derived fixtures.

- **Export schema 3.0** — the downloaded JSON/CSV now carries only what a reviewer reads: `id`, `permalink` (always present), `category`, `confidence`, `score`, `evidence`, `signals.email`, `signals.jobLink`, `author`, `extracted` (emails, job titles, hashtags, links), `postAge.label`/`postAge.publishedAt`, and `fullText`. Removed from the file: `sourcePostId`, `reviewLabel`, `firstSeenAt`, `lastSeenAt`, `metricsCapturedAt`, `historyStore`, `historyStores`, `contentComplete`, `extractionStatus`, `metrics` (reactions/comments/reposts/views), `postAge.ageSecondsApprox`, `postAge.ageDaysApprox`, and 12 of the 14 `signals` flags. The **Age and metrics** export toggle became **Post age** and now localizes in all three locales, and a stored `includeMetrics: false` is still honoured. Nothing was removed from the records the extension keeps — deduplication, retention, history merging, ordering and the result rows use the full internal shape. Field reference: `docs/export-schema.md`.

### Changed

- Consolidated adaptive scrolling into three modes while retaining Super Fast’s 15-minute/120-step caps and locked pause-on-input.
- Scoped content scripts to LinkedIn root/feed routes with Chrome 110 as the tested manifest floor.
- Prevented comment controls and uncertain/non-hiring partial cards from entering expansion or detected-history workflows.
- Canonicalized LinkedIn identity/URLs and retained complete text when merging newer partial observations.
- Preserved result focus/selection across identical refreshes and separated independent scroll/monitor command generations.
- Renamed user-facing modes to Adaptive naming and documented hidden-tab throttling and DOM-compatibility limitations.
- Result order now defaults to **Active openings first** (openings, then recruiter searches, possible matches, job seekers, advice, closed — freshest post first inside each category) while **Newest first** and **Oldest first** stay purely chronological. No category is hidden and no classifier signal changed.
- Removed the **Partial text** result filter. Partial-only records are deliberately never archived, so that option could only ever render an empty list; partial content is still flagged on the records that are archived.
- The popup now uses Chrome's real popup ceiling instead of a narrow column: 780px wide with a 560–600px body, a larger type scale (15px base, 13px minimum, headings 22/18/16), 44px-tall controls, and roomier panels. Above 720px the extra width is spent on columns rather than stretched: the scroll status and actions span the width with the pace and limits panels side by side, Start sits beside Pause/Stop, detection controls sit beside results, search and result order share a row, metrics go 2×2, and export fields go three-up. Below 720px the single-column flow and the 360px reflow are unchanged, and the accessibility invariants (focus-visible, reduced motion, RTL, contrast tokens) are untouched.
- Post-root selection now treats only card-level elements as cards. `feed-shared-update-v2__description-wrapper`, `__content`, `__actor`, and `__social-actions` are parts of a card, not cards, and a nested card is a separate post only when it carries its own distinct post URN (a reshare).

### Fixed

- One real LinkedIn post no longer produces several archive records. Nested card children used to become independent post roots with `text:` identities while the card kept its `urn:` identity, so a single post could appear three times, inflate `counts.open_role` and the results total, burn the export-archive limit, and report contradictory complete/partial labels.
- Page chrome no longer enters post text: see-more/see-less and comment control labels, and — in card-level fallback — actor, timestamp, action row, and control-menu text are excluded from the text that is classified, deduplicated, displayed, and exported.
- Opening-shaped intake phrasing (`applications are open`, `join our team`, `our team is growing`, `come work with us`, `referrals welcome`, `candidatures ouvertes`, `التقديم مفتوح`, `فرصة عمل`) no longer reaches **Openings** on its own, which removed programme, webinar, grant, entrance-exam, volunteering, and employer-praise false positives. It needs independent employment wording or a job/careers link, and otherwise stays in **Possible matches**.
- Added structural vacancy frames instead of title families: `Hiring: <title>` / `Hiring - <title>` headlines and the Arabic `مطلوب … للعمل|للانضمام|في` frame, plus `je recrute` and `nous sommes à la recherche`. These recovered common EN/FR/AR vacancies that were previously reported as no signal at all.
- A phrase can no longer corroborate itself: employment wording counted as context must be independent of the matched opening phrase (`فرصة عمل` contains `عمل`).
- Negation is scoped to its own clause. Because commas normalize to spaces, a negation used to suppress any genuine cue within four words — “I am not hiring, but here are hiring tips …” was reported as no signal with empty evidence, and an Arabic “we are not hiring, but applications are open …” was reported as closed. Connectives and sentence boundaries now end the negation’s scope.
- Suppressed job-seeker, advice/news, intake, and vacancy-frame signals are now reported as `Negated …` evidence entries, so a “No hiring signal” result stays explainable instead of silently discarding what matched.
- `نبحث عن مرشحين` moved from active-opening to recruiter-search phrasing, so an Arabic candidate search is no longer reported as an opening by the same words.
- Negated and mixed-polarity hiring phrases no longer become positive openings without independent positive evidence.
- CSV exports neutralize spreadsheet-formula-leading untrusted cells.
- Concurrent history updates are serialized instead of overwriting another tab’s records.
- Enabled retention is enforced on initialization and every history/export read path.
- Feed/result refreshes no longer apply stale responses after target changes or cancel unrelated commands.
- Extension-owned hidden feed attributes/styles are removed on stop and lifecycle cleanup.

- **Real-export defect — post identity and deduplication.** Cards in the live 2026-10-22 feed carry no `data-urn` or `data-id`, so all 32 exported posts received a volatile `text:` identity (`sourcePostId: null` on 32/32) and the same post could not be recognized across runs. Identity now falls back to the post URN found in the card's own attributes, descendant nodes or permalink anchors, and a permalink is derived from that URN when no anchor canonicalizes (permalinks were null on 26/32). `text:` stays the last resort, now computed over chrome-free text.
- **Real-export defect — chrome in classified text.** `innerText` is empty for occluded cards (`content-visibility`), so the raw-`textContent` fallback pulled hidden accessibility labels (“Post du fil d'actualité” opened every text), actor headlines, timestamps, follow/like/comment/permalink/translate labels, reaction counts and comment bodies into the text that is classified, deduplicated, displayed and exported. Extraction now clones the body node and removes card chrome — actor blocks, control menus, social-action bars, comment threads, sponsored labels, buttons and visually-hidden nodes — before reading it, and reports `partial`, `usedCardFallback`, `sponsored` and `fullTextAvailableWithoutExpansion` next to the text.
- **Real-export defect — authorship.** The actor name was read from the whole card and then rejected as a generic label, so `author.name` was null on 32/32 records, and a reactor wrapper credited a shared post to whoever liked it. Detected content now resolves to the innermost real content scope (the reshare/reactor container when one is present), and identity, permalink, author and engagement counts are all read from that scope.
- **Real-export defect — `contentComplete: false` on every post.** All 32 records reported truncated text because the body selector list did not include the `update-components-text` family current LinkedIn serves, so extraction fell back to the whole card and every application instruction after the “see more” marker was silently lost. The body is now found in the real markup, and the flag keeps its strict meaning: `false` only when a see-more control is present or no body node matched.
- **Real-export defect — post age.** Absolute timestamps (“18 oct.”, “27 sept.”, “Oct 18”, Arabic month names) were unreadable, so `postAge.label` was null on 31/32 records, and one record reported the age label `and` because the relative parser matched an ordinary English word through its single-letter unit alternation. EN/FR/AR absolute dates now parse to a real `publishedAt` (12:00 UTC, rolled back one year when the date would be in the future), and relative units require a preceding digit.
- **Real-export defect — advertising classified as a post.** A sponsored LinkedIn Jobs placement was archived as a possible opening because no advertising rule existed. Sponsored cards are now detected from the card itself (`promo`/`sponsored` markup or a sponsored label) and reported to the classifier, which records the `sponsored` signal and refuses to read advertising copy as a vacancy.
- **Real-export defect — engagement counts.** Counts were searched across the whole card, so a reshare's or a comment's counts could be attributed to the post. Metrics are now read from the post's own content scope, prefer its social-actions region and exclude comment threads. Counts are still never inferred from bare numbers: the export showed those bare numbers are dates and follower counts as often as engagement.
- Classifier accuracy on real text: over the 26 sanitized posts from that export the shipped run was correct on 13 (accuracy 0.500) and is now correct on 26 (1.000) — four unambiguous vacancies moved `possible`→`open_role`, commentary and intake moved `open_role`→`hiring_discussion`/`possible`, one job-seeker post moved `possible`→`job_seeker`, and advertising and non-hiring posts moved `possible`→`none`.
